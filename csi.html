Our Commitment

Why Corporate Social Investment

At Marula Fund Managers, we believe that true investment success extends beyond financial returns. Our Corporate Social Investment (CSI) initiatives reflect our deep commitment to creating lasting positive change in the communities where we operate.

We recognise that sustainable economic growth in Africa requires more than capital—it requires investment in people, skills, culture, and community infrastructure. Our CSI portfolio is carefully curated to support organisations that demonstrate measurable impact, sustainable practices, and alignment with our values of empowerment, education, and cultural preservation.

Our Portfolio

CSI Portfolio

Our CSI portfolio comprises non-profit organisations (NPCs) that share our vision for sustainable community development and measurable social impact.

Established 2002

Thembalethu Development NPC

Community Upliftment & Socio-Economic Empowerment

A prominent Southern African non-profit organisation focused on community upliftment, sustainable livelihoods, and socio-economic empowerment across all nine provinces of South Africa. Headquartered in Johannesburg, the organisation has managed over R1.8 billion in funds and averages 33 community project sites annually.

Core Focus Areas

Agriculture & Food Security

Community gardens, agricultural training, and farming productivity

Education & Skills

School building, ECD centres, and ABET learning schemes

Community Health Care

Trained personnel in rural and underserved healthcare facilities

Economic Development

SEF & CWP partnerships, employment, and infrastructure

Environment & Responsible Mining

Waste collection, recycling, composting systems, and social labour plans in mining

Lekgoledi Arts and Cultural Centre (LACC)

Arts, Culture & Heritage Development

A women- and youth-led non-profit organisation based in Limpopo Province, operating out of Lebowakgomo and Tooseng. LACC focuses on community research, project conceptualisation, youth skills development, and growing the rural creative, cultural, and heritage arts sector in Limpopo.

Key Programmes

Regional Brass Band

Youth brass band development and performances

Art Masterclasses

Youth animation and art skills development

Digital Gallery Projects

Digital preservation and promotion of local art
