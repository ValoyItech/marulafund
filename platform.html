Every stage must improve the quality of the investment case. Weak assumptions are surfaced early. Strong opportunities are advanced toward execution with structure and confidence.

Quality Control

Platform Decision Gates

Every project must pass through rigorous decision gates before advancing. This ensures only the strongest, most viable opportunities reach capital markets.

Fit

Sector, mandate, sponsor and partner alignment assessment.

Viability

Commercial, technical, market, regulatory and ESG logic.

Bankability

Funder requirements, evidence quality and risk allocation.

Execution

Permits, procurement, contracts, capacity and controls.

Value

Portfolio performance, resilience and strategic growth.

Our Standard

Platform Discipline

A project should enter the funding market with clear logic, credible numbers, allocated risk, funder-ready documents and a delivery path that can survive scrutiny.

Marula's preparation model is designed to close the gap between concept and capital. Projects fail when strategy, finance, contracts, delivery and governance are treated as separate exercises. Our integrated approach ensures every element is aligned before a project reaches funders.

Clear investment logic

Every project has a defined mandate, market case and return profile before capital is sought.

Credible financial models

Auditable models with sensitivity testing, scenario analysis and lender review.

Allocated risk framework

Risks identified, priced and assigned to parties best able to manage them.

Funder-ready documentation

Complete evidence packs, governance structures and implementation plans.
