Who We Are

Marula Fund Managers is an investment holding and strategic project development company focused on identifying, preparing, structuring, supporting and scaling high-potential African businesses across selected real economy sectors.

We combine capital mobilisation, project preparation, strategic partnerships and operational capability to transform high-potential opportunities into sustainable businesses. Our work spans mining, agriculture, aquaculture, technology, infrastructure, security and business development.
